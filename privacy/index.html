Collabo is an app that helps film and video people build a profile of work they can prove, find projects and crew up. This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and the choices you have. We've kept it short and plain.

Who we are

Collabo is run by Studio for Good, trading as collabo. Studio for Good is Taiwo T's sole-trader business in the UK. It isn't a limited company. For this policy, Studio for Good is the "controller" of your personal data, which means it decides how and why your data is used.

The short version

  • We collect what you give us to make your profile, post projects, apply, earn credits and talk to other members.
  • Your profile and projects can be seen by other members. Your email address and password can't.
  • We don't sell your data, don't show ads and don't use analytics or tracking tools.
  • Our main service provider is Supabase, which hosts the app's database, files and sign-in.
  • You can delete your account, and you can ask us for a copy of your data at any time.

What we collect

Your account

  • Email address and password. Your password is stored scrambled (hashed) by our sign-in provider, and we can't see it.
  • Your confirmation that you're 18 or over, and when you gave it.
  • Whether you've confirmed your email, and basic sign-in records.

Your profile (other members can see this)

  • Full name, username, location (the town or city you type in), bio and the roles you work in, such as Director or Gaffer.
  • Optional links: Instagram, YouTube, Vimeo, a website, and portfolio links such as showreels and stills.
  • Your credits (body of work): the project title, type, year and your role on it, with a verified or pending badge.
  • Your rank for each role, worked out from your verified credits.
  • There's no profile photo in this version. Profiles show your initials.

Projects you post (other members can see these)

  • Title, type, description, location, shoot dates and the roles you need.
  • Your visual treatment (a PDF or 1 to 6 images) and a cover image.

Applications and crew

  • The roles you apply for, any note you add, and whether you were invited, accepted, declined or withdrew.
  • Who is on each project's crew.

Proof for credits

  • To verify a credit, you or a project owner upload proof, such as an end-credits screenshot, a file or a link (for example to IMDb). Proof is seen by the Collabo admin for review. It isn't shown publicly.

Messages

  • Messages you send in project threads and project or department chats, along with who sent them and when.

Safety records

  • Reports you make (what you reported, your reason and its status) and the people you've blocked.
  • Moderation decisions, such as approving or rejecting a credit (with a reason), hiding content or suspending an account.

Technical information

  • When you sign in, the app stores a sign-in token on your phone so you stay signed in.
  • Our hosting provider keeps standard server and security logs. These can include your IP address, device or browser type, and the time of each request.
  • If you choose a treatment or cover image, the app asks for access to your photos. It only uses the photos you pick, and doesn't use your camera or microphone.

Things we don't collect

  • We don't use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and we don't collect your precise location.
  • We don't collect special category data, such as health or ethnicity. Please don't put it in your bio, posts or messages.

Who can see your data

Other members. Signed-in members can see your profile, credits, rank, projects you post and the crew lists you're on. Messages are only visible to the people in that thread or chat. Your email address, password, credit proof, reports and blocks aren't shown to other members.

Once share links are available in the app, if you share a link to your profile or a project, anyone with the link can see a basic web page for it at trycollabo.app. Once the profile privacy setting is available, Settings will let you choose whether your profile is visible to everyone or only to members. Drafts, hidden content and people you've blocked are never shared.

The Collabo admin. Our admin reviews credit proof, project completions and reports, and can see account data when needed to run, support and moderate the service.

International transfers

Your data is hosted in the EU, which the UK recognises as giving adequate protection. Where a provider may access it from outside the UK or EU, such as Supabase in the US, we rely on safeguards approved under UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in the provider's data processing agreement.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and profile: while your account is open. When you delete your account, your profile, roles, links, applications, messages you sent, reports you made, blocks, and projects you own are deleted.
  • Credits on other people's profiles: if you delete a project you owned, verified credits that your crew earned on it stay on their profiles, with the project's title, type and year, because that's their work history.
  • Unfinished project drafts: once automatic clean-up is available, drafts left unchanged for 7 days are deleted automatically.
  • Moderation and safety records: kept for TBC after the action, so we can deal with repeat abuse and disputes.
  • Server and security logs: kept for the hosting provider's standard period.
  • Backups: deleted data may stay in backups for up to TBC before it's overwritten.
  • Support emails: kept for TBC after your query is resolved.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Access your data and get a copy of it
  • Correct data that's wrong. You can edit most of your profile in the app.
  • Delete your data. Once account deletion is available in the app, you can delete your account there. Until then, email us.
  • Restrict how we use your data
  • Object to uses based on legitimate interests
  • Data portability: get the data you gave us in a common format

To use any of these rights, email collaboapp.company@gmail.com from the address on your account. We'll reply within one month. It's free, unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first so we can try to fix it. You also have the right to complain to the UK regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.

Over 18s only

Collabo is only for people aged 18 or over. You have to confirm you're 18 or over when you sign up. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we'll delete it. If you think a child is using Collabo, email us.

Security

Access to data is controlled at the database level, so members can only change their own data and only the admin can see the review queue. Connections are encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, though. Use a strong password that you don't use anywhere else, and tell us straight away if you think your account has been compromised.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy when the app changes, for example when direct messages between any members, an activity feed or notifications launch. We'll change the "Last updated" date, and if a change is significant we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
